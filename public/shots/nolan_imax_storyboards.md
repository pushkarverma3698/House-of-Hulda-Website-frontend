# House of Hulda: Christopher Nolan IMAX Stargazing Experience — Storyboards & Shot Specs

> **Review & Audit Note**: Our initial pass over-indexed on isolated atmospheric mist plates (black background luma-keys). That approach was sterile and lost the emotional magic of the guest's journey. 
> 
> Below is the **Full Cinematic Storyboard**, mapping the actual narrative shots (15:40 Afternoon Drive to 06:05 Himalayan Dawn). We use real IMAX 70mm keyframe images as the Start & End anchors for video generation.

---

## Shot 1: The Road Ends (15:40 - Valley Drive to Rumsu Trailhead)

*   **Vibe**: Interstellar / Dunkirk 70mm tracking shot. Claustrophobic 85mm compression into the ancient Deodar canopy.
*   **Start Frame (`public/shots/shot_1_mist/start.jpg`)**:
    IMAX 70mm tracking shot from inside a classic 4x4 vehicle driving along a winding mountain pass in Naggar/Rumsu. Overcast afternoon sky, snow peaks in the distance.
*   **End Frame Narrative (`public/shots/shot_1_mist/end.jpg`)**:
    The road stops abruptly at the edge of Rumsu village. Atmospheric mountain mist creeps across the dirt trail as ancient pine trees close overhead.
*   **Video Motion Prompt (Veo 3 / Kling / Midjourney / Flux)**:
    > "Cinematic 70mm IMAX tracking shot pushing slowly forward along a rugged mountain road in Naggar Himalayas at 15:40 afternoon light. Dense Deodar pine trees towering on both sides, cold valley mist slowly creeping across the dirt path, snow-capped peaks in the background. Christopher Nolan color grade, moody, realistic, 24fps."
